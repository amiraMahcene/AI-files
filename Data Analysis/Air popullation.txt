The problem : City Air Pollution Analysis 

A city wants to reduce air  pollution and understand its causes, patterns, and potential solutions

Step 01  :  Understand the problem

Ask question : 

 * what are the main sources of air pollution
 * which areas are most affected ? 
 * at what times or seasons does pollution peak? 
 * who is most impacted ( Children, elderly, etc..)
 
 - Air quality Index
 - Number of coffee shops in the city
 - PM2.5 concentration
 - Number of students enrolled in local schools
 - Daily traffic volume
 - Temperature and humidity 
 - Brand of cars owned by residents
 - Wind speed
 - Number of parks and green areas
 - Internet speed in households
 - Factory emission levels
 - Availability of public transport
 - Number of music events per month 
 - Burning of waste in open areas
 - Rainfall levels
 - Population density
 - Weekly cinema attendance 
 - Use of bicycles by citizen
 - Construction activities
 - Hospital admission due to respiratory issues
